Biometric Authentication

Log in with Face ID or fingerprint. Spensr uses your device's biometric hardware so your financial data is protected by your unique identity.

Authentication and real-time sync are handled by Firebase — Google's enterprise-grade backend platform with built-in security rules and abuse protection.

Stored in MongoDB Atlas

All your expense data lives in MongoDB Atlas — a fully managed cloud database with encryption at rest, automated backups, and global availability.
